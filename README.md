One kind, funny fortune for a houseplant, written as if the plant itself were
having its future told — for the [Fortune in a Pot](https://make.fun/bounty/u-golden-heron-44-fortune-in-a-pot-ded981e9) bounty on make.fun.

## The fortune

> Tomorrow the sun will find your newest leaf first, and your keeper will smile at you like you planned it that way.

It is a prediction, not a care tip: tomorrow's sun, the plant's newest leaf,
and a keeper who smiles — with one small joke (the plant planned it) that a
plant lover would laugh at, and nothing but warmth for plant and keeper. It
is written for this plant, in this window, not for any plant in any room.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) writes the single fortune line to
`/output/entry.txt`. The line is 114 characters: one line, UTF-8, inside the
bounty's 1–120 character gate.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
